Library terms

https://draftsman-nomen.github.io/test-repo/ · ontology.ttl · 494e7f7

  • book
    A book is a written work that the library lists once in its catalogue, however many copies of it there are.
  • copy
    A copy is a physical object of a printed book that the library owns and lends out.
  • due date
    A due date is the date by which a loan must be returned.
  • e-book
    An e-book is a book that you read on a screen, and that more than one member can borrow at the same time.
  • fine
    A fine is an amount that a member pays for each day that a loan is overdue.
  • loan
    A loan is an agreement that a member may keep a copy or an e-book until its due date.
  • member
    A member is a person who is registered with the library and may borrow from it.
  • overdue
    Overdue is the state of a loan whose due date has passed while the copy has not been returned.
  • printed book
    A printed book is a book that exists on paper, and of which the library owns one or more copies.
  • renewal
    A renewal is a change to a loan that moves its due date later.
  • reservation
    A reservation is a request by a member to borrow a book as soon as a copy of it is back from loan.
  • youth member
    A youth member is a member who is younger than eighteen.
