e-book

https://draftsman-nomen.github.io/test-repo/e-book

Preferred term
e-book
Alternative terms
digital book
Definition
An e-book is a book that you read on a screen, and that more than one member can borrow at the same time.
Example
A novel that members download to an e-reader for three weeks.
Broader concept
book
Status
In use
